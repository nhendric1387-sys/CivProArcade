    How to play Level 1

    1. A rule, statute, or model rule will appear. Read the prompt.
    2. Type the number that goes with it, plus the period key for ABA Model Rules with decimals (like 3.1).
    3. Press ENTER to submit your answer. Backspace fixes a typo first.
    4. Press SPACEBAR to skip a question you don't know — it comes back around at the end.
    5. Wrong answers cost nothing but time, so keep trying.
    6. You have 60 seconds. However many you get right is your score.
    7. You get 3 attempts each visit — every attempt posts your score to the leaderboard.
